# Product Guidelines

## Documentation & Communication
- **Tone:** Use a **Hands-on and Practical** tone that is also **Conversational and Approachable**. The goal is to make complex distributed systems concepts accessible while providing immediate utility for developers.
- **Code Commenting Strategy:** Focus on:
    - **The "Why":** Explain design decisions and the rationale behind choosing specific Orleans patterns (e.g., grain activation, state management).
    - **The "How":** Detail the mechanics and sequence of operations within the MapReduce pipeline.
    - **Integration Details:** Highlight the interactions and communication flows between the Client, Silo, and Grains.

## Educational Content Structure
- **README & Guides:** Structure documentation to facilitate iterative learning:
    - **Quick Start First:** Provide immediate, actionable instructions to run the Silo and Client.
    - **Iterative Depth:** Gradually introduce more complex Orleans features and architectural deep dives after the initial success of running the project.
    - **Architecture Deep Dives:** Include detailed sections explaining the actor model and MapReduce logic as users progress.
